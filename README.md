# Research areas
I have long research history in signal processing (especially audio, speech), machine learning and natural language processing in applications to consumer electronics, and healthcare. 

My current most active application areas are 
- Analysis and optimization of large transformer models, see relevant papers [here](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=nl&user=ONX81n4AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate). 
- Educational technology, especially use of conversational systems (text/speech) in education, see [various ACAbot projects](https://www.edudacs.nl/projects/67c19022748fbc62d93a576f). 
- Biodiversity monitoring and especially [Passive Acoustic Monitoring of populations of birds and other animals](https://ml4biodiversity.org).
- I am growing new research work in application of AI in sustainable agriculture. I have several activities in this domain wiht our colleagues at the [Brightlands Future of Farming Insitute in Venlo.](https://www.brightlands.com/en/campus-greenport-venlo/expertises/future-farming). 

I am also always open for great ideas from students, for example, in areas of language agents and assistants, audio and music applications, and sustainable agriculture and climate change.
